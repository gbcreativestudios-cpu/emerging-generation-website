Aisha Yahaya is a finance professional, youth coach, mentor, facilitator, and passionate advocate for the emerging generation. With a Master’s in Finance and professional experience spanning finance, banking, nonprofit organizations, and community development, Aisha brings a unique blend of analytical thinking, lived experience, and a deep passion for personal transformation.



Through her work mentoring and coaching young people, Aisha creates safe and engaging spaces where youth can explore identity, purpose, confidence, leadership, and the challenges that come with navigating different seasons of life.



She is also the host of The Arise Podcast with Aisha, a faith-centered platform created to inspire everyday people to rise into the fullness of who God created them to be. Through conversations, stories, reflections, and practical insights, Aisha explores themes of identity, purpose, growth, and transformation.



Aisha is passionate about helping young people recognize that their identity is not defined by their circumstances, environment, culture, or the expectations of others but by discovering and embracing who they are and who they are becoming.
